# Ex.No 5 — Demo Documents

These fictional sample documents are provided for testing the AI Document Analyzer.

Files:
- `sample_contract.pdf` — sample legal-style agreement in PDF format
- `sample_contract.docx` — the same fictional agreement in Word format
- `sample_literary_story.docx` — short story with characters, plot, themes, and moral
- `sample_project_memo.txt` — plain-text memo with recommendations and risks

Suggested test questions:
- What is the monthly rent and when is it due?
- What are the confidentiality and termination clauses?
- Who are the main characters in the story?
- What is the moral lesson?
- What are the memo's key recommendations and risks?

All content is fictional, created for educational software testing, and is not legal advice.
