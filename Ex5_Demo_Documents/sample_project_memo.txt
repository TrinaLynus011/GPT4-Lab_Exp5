PROJECT MEMORANDUM
Subject: Improving Document Review Workflow
Date: 6 November 2026
From: Document Operations Team (fictional)

Purpose
This memo proposes a simple workflow for reviewing incoming documents and reducing repetitive manual work.

Key recommendations
1. Store each document with a descriptive filename and date.
2. Extract text from digital PDF, DOCX, and TXT files before review.
3. Create a concise summary highlighting purpose, key facts, and action items.
4. Record questions that cannot be answered from the document.
5. Verify generated summaries against the source before sharing them.
6. Avoid uploading confidential or personal information to unapproved services.

Expected benefits
The workflow may improve consistency, reduce review time, and make important information easier to locate. AI-generated outputs should remain drafts and must be checked by a human reviewer.

Risks and limitations
Scanned PDFs may require OCR. Poorly formatted files can produce incomplete extraction. AI systems may omit details or generate inaccurate statements, so users should verify all outputs.

Conclusion
A document analysis tool can support the review process when used with careful human verification and appropriate data handling.
